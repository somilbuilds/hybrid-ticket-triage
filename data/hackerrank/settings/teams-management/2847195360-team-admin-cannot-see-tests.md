# Team Admin Cannot See Tests

_Last updated: September 4, 2026, 1:55 PM (Last updated 13 days ago)_

This article explains what to check when a team administrator cannot see tests in HackerRank for Work. Test visibility can depend on team membership, ownership, and assigned permissions.

# Prerequisites

- The user must be a member of the expected team.
- You must have permission to manage team membership or roles.
- Identify the tests the user should be able to access.

# Checking test visibility

To troubleshoot missing tests:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Settings** and select **Teams**.
3. Open the team associated with the administrator.
4. Verify that the user is a member of the correct team.
5. Review the user's assigned role and permissions.
6. Confirm that the affected tests are owned by or shared with the relevant team, where applicable.
7. Open **Tests** and check whether filters are hiding the expected tests.
8. Clear or change the active filters and search for the test by name.
9. Ask the user to refresh the page or sign in again after permission changes.

**Note:** Being a team administrator does not necessarily provide access to tests belonging to another team or resources that have not been shared with the user's team.

If the user still cannot see the tests, compare their role and team membership with another administrator who has the expected access. See [📄 Role Permission Missing](/articles/9625483170).
