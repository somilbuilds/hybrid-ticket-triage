# Invite and Manage Team Members

_Last updated: September 3, 2026, 10:30 AM (Last updated 2 weeks ago)_

Administrators can invite team members and assign access based on their responsibilities. Review existing users before sending invitations to avoid duplicate accounts.

# Prerequisites

- Organization administrator or user-management permissions.
- The team member's business email address.
- An appropriate role for the team member.

# Add a team member

To invite a team member:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Settings** and select **User Management**.
3. Select **Invite User**.
4. Enter the team member's email address.
5. Select the appropriate **Role** or permission level.
6. Review the access granted by the selected role.
7. Select **Send Invite**.
8. Return to **User Management** to confirm the user appears with the expected status.

**Note:** Assign only the permissions required for the user's responsibilities. If an existing account uses the same email address, update that account instead of creating a duplicate invitation.

For missing permissions, see [📄 Role Permission Missing](/articles/9625483170).
