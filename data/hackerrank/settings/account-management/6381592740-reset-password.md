# Reset Password

_Last updated: August 27, 2026, 10:25 AM (Last updated 21 days ago)_

You can reset your HackerRank for Work password when you cannot remember your current password. This workflow applies to accounts that use password-based authentication.

# Prerequisites

- You must know the email address associated with your account.
- Your account must use password authentication.
- You must have access to the email inbox associated with the account.

# Resetting your password

To reset your password:

1. Open the HackerRank for Work sign-in page.
2. Select **Forgot Password**.
3. Enter the email address associated with your HackerRank for Work account.
4. Submit the password reset request.
5. Open the password reset email sent to your registered email address.
6. Select the reset link in the email.
7. Enter and confirm your new password.
8. Save the new password and return to the sign-in page.
9. Sign in using your email address and new password.

**Note:** Password reset is not available for users whose organization requires single sign-on authentication. If your organization uses SSO, contact your organization's administrator if you cannot authenticate.

If you do not receive the reset email, check your spam or junk folder and confirm that you entered the correct email address. If your account is locked or disabled, see [📄 User Locked Out](/articles/8624159375).
