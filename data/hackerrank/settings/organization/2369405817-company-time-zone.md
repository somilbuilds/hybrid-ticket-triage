# Set the Company Time Zone

_Last updated: August 16, 2026, 10:05 AM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

The organization time zone can affect how scheduled activities, dates, and reporting timestamps are displayed. Set it to the time zone your team uses for its operating workflows.

# Prerequisites

- Organization administrator access to **HackerRank for Work**.
- The intended company time zone.
- Awareness of any active assessments or scheduled interviews.

# Update the time zone

To update the organization time zone:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Settings** and select the organization or company settings.
3. Locate the **Time Zone** setting.
4. Review the currently configured time zone.
5. Select the required time zone from the available options.
6. Select **Save**.
7. Verify a scheduled resource or report to confirm that displayed times are consistent with the new setting.

**Note:** Changing an organization time zone can affect how future dates and times are displayed. Confirm the change with teams that manage interviews or time-sensitive assessments before updating it.

For test expiration settings, see [📄 Test Time Limit and Expiration Settings](/articles/8263517492).
