# Transfer Resource Ownership

_Last updated: September 2, 2026, 10:35 AM (Last updated 15 days ago)_

You can transfer ownership of supported HackerRank for Work resources to another user in your organization. This is useful when the current owner changes roles or leaves the organization.

# Prerequisites

- You must have permission to manage the resource.
- The new owner must belong to your HackerRank for Work organization.
- Confirm that the resource should be transferred to the selected user.

# Transferring ownership

To transfer ownership:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to the area containing the resource you want to transfer.
3. Open the resource and select the available **Settings** or **More** options.
4. Locate the **Owner** or **Ownership** field.
5. Select the user who should become the new owner.
6. Review the resource details and selected owner.
7. Click **Save** or **Transfer Ownership**.
8. Verify that the new owner's name appears on the resource.

**Note:** The new owner must have access to the organization and any required product area. Transferring ownership does not necessarily change other permissions or team membership.

If the ownership option is unavailable, verify your role and the resource type. For user and role management, see [📄 Managing Users and Roles](/articles/3617952048).
