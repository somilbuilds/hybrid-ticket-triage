# Manage Notification Preferences

_Last updated: August 15, 2026, 4:15 PM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

Notification preferences control which account or workflow emails a user receives. Review these settings when users receive unnecessary notifications or expected messages are not being delivered.

# Prerequisites

- Access to your **HackerRank for Work** account.
- Permission to change personal or organization notification settings.
- Knowledge of which notification types your workflow requires.

# Update notification preferences

To manage notifications:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Settings** and locate **Notifications**.
3. Review the available notification categories.
4. Identify the notifications that you want to receive or disable.
5. Update the relevant preference settings.
6. Select **Save** when prompted.
7. Trigger or wait for a supported notification event to verify the expected behavior.

**Note:** Some workflow notifications may be controlled by organization-level settings or by the action that generates the message. A personal preference may not override an organization-wide requirement.

If expected emails are missing, see [📄 Email Notifications Not Received](/articles/9698052496).
