# Set Reply-To Email

_Last updated: September 4, 2026, 2:20 PM (Last updated 13 days ago)_

You can configure a reply-to email address for supported HackerRank for Work notifications so candidate responses are directed to the appropriate recruiting or hiring team.

# Prerequisites

- You must have permission to manage organization email settings.
- Confirm the email address that should receive candidate replies.
- The address must be valid and accessible to your organization.

# Configuring the reply-to address

To set a reply-to email address:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Settings** and open **Email Settings**.
3. Locate the **Reply-To Email** field.
4. Enter the email address that should receive replies to supported notifications.
5. Review the address for spelling and formatting errors.
6. Click **Save** to apply the setting.
7. Send or trigger a test notification if the option is available.
8. Verify that replies are routed to the configured address.

**Note:** The reply-to setting affects supported HackerRank for Work emails and does not necessarily change the sender address shown on an email. Organization or domain-level email policies may also affect message delivery.

If replies continue to go to the previous address, verify that the notification type supports the configured reply-to address and that the setting was saved successfully. See [📄 Configure Company Email Settings](/articles/6251837490).
