# Change Company Logo

_Last updated: September 1, 2026, 11:10 AM (Last updated 16 days ago)_

You can update the company logo displayed in HackerRank for Work. Company branding settings are generally available to users with organization administration permissions.

# Prerequisites

- You must have permission to manage company settings.
- Prepare the logo image in a supported format and suitable dimensions.
- Confirm that you want the new logo to replace the existing company branding.

# Updating the company logo

To change the company logo:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Settings** and open **Company Profile** or the organization branding settings.
3. Locate the current company logo and select **Change** or **Upload**.
4. Choose the new logo file from your device.
5. Review the logo preview.
6. Click **Save** or **Update** to apply the new branding.
7. Refresh the application to verify that the updated logo is displayed.

**Note:** Branding changes can take a short time to appear across previously opened pages. If the old logo remains visible, refresh the page or sign out and sign in again.

If you cannot find the company branding settings, verify that your account has the required administrator permissions. For other organization settings, see [📄 Managing Company Settings](/articles/3817294605).
