# Test Time Limit and Expiration Settings

_Last updated: September 8, 2026, 2:20 PM (Last updated 1 week ago)_

Use test timing settings to control how long candidates can work on an assessment and when an invitation stops being usable.

# Prerequisites

- Test management access in **HackerRank for Work**.
- An existing test that you want to configure.
- The intended assessment duration and availability window.

# Configure timing settings

To review or update test timing:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Tests** and select the assessment you want to configure.
3. Open **Test Settings**.
4. Locate the settings for the candidate's **Time Limit** and invitation or test **Expiration**.
5. Enter the intended duration and availability period.
6. Review any existing candidate-specific extensions before saving.
7. Select **Save** to apply the changes.
8. Send or resend invitations only after confirming the final timing configuration.

**Note:** A test's availability window and a candidate's allowed test duration control different parts of the experience. A candidate who starts close to the expiration boundary may be affected by the configured expiration behavior.

For candidate-specific extensions, see [📄 Extend Time After Test Started](/articles/3157048621).
