# Candidate Report Missing

_Last updated: September 4, 2026, 11:30 AM (Last updated 13 days ago)_

This article explains how to troubleshoot a missing candidate report after a HackerRank for Work test has been completed. Check the candidate status, test attempt, and report permissions before contacting support.

# Prerequisites

- The candidate must have a completed or submitted test attempt.
- You must have permission to view candidate reports.
- Confirm that the correct test and candidate are selected.

# Checking the candidate report

To locate a missing report:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Tests** and open the relevant test.
3. Select **Candidates** and search for the candidate.
4. Review the candidate's **Status** and confirm that the test has been submitted.
5. Open the candidate's assessment details and check whether the report is available.
6. Refresh the page and allow processing to complete if the submission was made recently.
7. Verify that your account has permission to view candidate results and reports.
8. If available, use **Reports** or **Download Report** to retrieve the completed assessment.

**Note:** A report may not be available immediately after submission while the assessment results are being processed. A candidate who has only saved progress without submitting the test may also not have a completed report.

If the candidate shows as completed but the report remains unavailable, record the candidate email, test name, submission time, and displayed status before contacting support. See [📄 Viewing Candidate Test Reports](/articles/6915284073).
