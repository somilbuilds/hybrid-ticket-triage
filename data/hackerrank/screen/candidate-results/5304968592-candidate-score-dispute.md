# Investigate a Candidate Score Dispute

_Last updated: July 22, 2026, 11:20 AM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

When a candidate questions an assessment score, review the recorded attempt, question-level results, and assessment configuration before changing or resetting the attempt.

# Prerequisites

- Permission to view candidate results.
- The candidate's test attempt.
- The assessment's scoring and question configuration.

# Review the score

To investigate a disputed result:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Tests** and select the relevant assessment.
3. Open **Candidates** and locate the candidate.
4. Open the candidate's completed attempt.
5. Review the overall score and question-level results.
6. Compare the result with the scoring rules and question weights configured for the test.
7. Review any available submission or evaluation details for the disputed question.
8. Record the findings before making changes to the candidate's attempt.

**Note:** Avoid modifying the assessment or candidate attempt while investigating unless required. A configuration change can make it harder to reproduce the original result.

For missing submissions, see [📄 Candidate Submission Is Missing](/articles/1814957362).
