# Candidate Score Not Calculated

_Last updated: August 22, 2026, 10:20 AM (Last updated 4 weeks ago)_

If a candidate has completed a test but no score is displayed, verify that the submission was recorded and that all questions have finished evaluation.

# Prerequisites

- Access to the relevant **HackerRank for Work** test.
- Permission to view candidate results.
- The candidate's email address or test attempt details.

# Check the candidate result

To investigate a missing score:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Tests** and select the relevant assessment.
3. Select **Candidates** and locate the candidate.
4. Open the candidate's test attempt.
5. Confirm that the attempt shows a completed or submitted status.
6. Review the individual question results and check whether any evaluation is still processing.
7. Refresh the result after pending evaluation has completed.
8. If the score remains unavailable, record the candidate, test, attempt time, and affected questions before contacting support.

**Note:** A completed candidate session and a finalized score are separate states. Some questions may require additional processing before the overall result is available.

For another result-related issue, see [📄 Candidate Report Missing](/articles/5379261840).
