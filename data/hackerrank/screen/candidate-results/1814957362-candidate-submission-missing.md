# Candidate Submission Is Missing

_Last updated: August 21, 2026, 3:35 PM (Last updated 4 weeks ago)_

When a candidate says they submitted an answer but the submission is not visible, check the attempt status, question history, and recorded activity before asking the candidate to retake the assessment.

# Prerequisites

- Access to the candidate's assessment.
- Permission to view submissions and candidate activity.
- The candidate's test attempt information.

# Review the submission

To investigate a missing submission:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open the relevant **Test** and select **Candidates**.
3. Search for the candidate and open the associated attempt.
4. Confirm that the attempt belongs to the expected candidate and test.
5. Open the affected question and review the available submission or activity history.
6. Check whether the candidate submitted code, saved a response, or exited before submitting.
7. Refresh the attempt and review the final test status.
8. If the submission is still missing, collect the candidate email, question name, approximate submission time, and browser details for investigation.

**Note:** Do not delete or reset the candidate attempt while investigating. Resetting an attempt can remove information that may be needed to diagnose the issue.

For candidate access issues, see [📄 Candidate Cannot Access a Test After Receiving an Invite](/articles/9152746381).
