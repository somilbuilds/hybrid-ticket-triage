# Export Candidate Results

_Last updated: August 5, 2026, 11:30 AM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

Export candidate results when you need to analyze assessment outcomes outside HackerRank for Work or share a controlled report with recruiting stakeholders.

# Prerequisites

- Permission to view candidate results.
- Access to the relevant test.
- A defined date, candidate group, or result scope to export.

# Export results

To export candidate results:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Tests** and select the assessment.
3. Select **Candidates** or the assessment results view.
4. Apply filters for candidate status, date, score, or other available criteria.
5. Review the filtered results and confirm that the correct candidates are included.
6. Select **Export** or the available download option.
7. Choose the supported output format when prompted.
8. Open the exported file and verify the candidate count and key fields before sharing it.

**Note:** Apply filters before exporting when the report contains sensitive candidate information. Store exported files according to your organization's recruiting and data-retention requirements.

For audit-related exports, see [📄 Audit Log Export](/articles/3958617240).
