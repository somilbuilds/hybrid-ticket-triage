# Candidate Cannot Access a Test After Receiving an Invite

_Last updated: September 10, 2026, 10:15 AM (Last updated 1 week ago)_

If a candidate received an invitation but cannot open the assessment, verify the invitation status, test availability, and candidate access settings before sending a new invitation.

# Prerequisites

- Access to the relevant **HackerRank for Work** test.
- Permission to manage candidates and invitations.
- The candidate's email address or invitation details.

# Check the candidate invitation

To verify whether the candidate can access the test:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open the relevant **Test** from the test library.
3. Select **Candidates** and search for the candidate by email address.
4. Check the invitation status and confirm that the invitation was sent to the correct email address.
5. Open the candidate's invitation details and verify that the test is still active.
6. Confirm that the candidate is using the same email address associated with the invitation.
7. If the invitation is valid, copy the current test link and ask the candidate to open it in a supported browser.

**Note:** If the test has expired, reached a configured access limit, or the candidate was removed from the assessment, sending the original link again will not restore access. Update the candidate's access settings or resend a valid invitation instead.

For browser-related checks, see [📄 Webcam and Proctoring Troubleshooting](/articles/6481372950).
