# Candidate Cannot See the Test Invitation

_Last updated: July 21, 2026, 9:40 AM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

If a candidate says they cannot find an assessment invitation, verify the invitation record and recipient address before sending another message.

# Prerequisites

- Candidate-management access in **HackerRank for Work**.
- The candidate's email address.
- Access to the relevant test.

# Check the invitation

To troubleshoot a missing invitation:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Tests** and select the assessment.
3. Select **Candidates** and search for the candidate.
4. Confirm that an invitation exists and was sent to the expected email address.
5. Check the invitation status and expiration date.
6. Ask the candidate to search spam, junk, quarantine, and filtered folders.
7. Confirm that the candidate's organization is not blocking the configured sender.
8. Resend the invitation only after verifying the recipient address and current test settings.

**Note:** If the candidate was invited using a personal or incorrect email address, sending additional copies to that same address may not resolve the issue. Correct the candidate record first.

For general email problems, see [📄 Email Notifications Not Received](/articles/9698052496).
