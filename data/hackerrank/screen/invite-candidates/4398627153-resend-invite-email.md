# Resend Invite Email

_Last updated: August 30, 2026, 11:05 AM (Last updated 18 days ago)_

You can resend a test invitation when a candidate did not receive the original email or needs a new invitation link. Verify the candidate's information and test availability before resending the invitation.

# Prerequisites

- You must have permission to manage candidates for the test.
- The candidate must be associated with the test.
- Confirm that the candidate's email address is correct.

# Resending the invitation

To resend a test invitation:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Tests** and open the required test.
3. Select **Candidates**.
4. Search for the candidate by name or email address.
5. Select the candidate's record.
6. Click **Resend Invite**.
7. Verify the displayed email address and invitation details.
8. Click **Send** to send the invitation again.
9. Ask the candidate to check their inbox, spam, and junk folders.

**Note:** Candidates should use the latest invitation email. Previous links may no longer work when an invitation has expired, been revoked, or replaced.

If the candidate does not receive the new email, verify the email address and check whether the candidate's mail provider is filtering messages from HackerRank. For access errors after receiving the invitation, see [📄 Test Link Says Unauthorized](/articles/6382051749).
