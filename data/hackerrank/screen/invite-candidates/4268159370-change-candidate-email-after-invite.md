# Change Candidate Email After Invite

_Last updated: August 29, 2026, 1:45 PM (Last updated 19 days ago)_

If a test invitation was sent to the wrong email address, you can update the candidate information and send a new invitation. Verify the candidate's status before making the change.

# Prerequisites

- You must have permission to manage candidates.
- Confirm the correct email address with the candidate or recruiting team.
- Check whether the candidate has already started the test.

# Updating the candidate email

To change the email address after an invitation has been sent:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Tests** and open the relevant test.
3. Select **Candidates** and locate the candidate.
4. Open the candidate's details and select the available **Edit** option.
5. Replace the existing value in **Email** with the correct address.
6. Click **Save** to update the candidate record.
7. Select **Resend Invite** to send the invitation to the new address.
8. Ask the candidate to use the latest invitation link.

**Note:** If the candidate has already started or completed the test, changing the email address may not transfer the existing attempt. Review the candidate's test status before changing identifying information.

The new invitation should be opened using the updated email address. If the candidate receives an Unauthorized message, see [📄 Test Link Says Unauthorized](/articles/6382051749).
