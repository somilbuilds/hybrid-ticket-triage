# Resend Invite Email

_Last updated: August 31, 2026, 2:05 PM (Last updated 17 days ago)_

You can resend a test invitation when a candidate did not receive the original email, deleted it, or needs a new active invitation link.

# Prerequisites

- You must have permission to manage candidates for the test.
- The candidate must already be associated with the test.
- Verify that the candidate's email address is correct.

# Resending a test invitation

To resend an invitation email:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Tests** and open the test for which you want to resend the invitation.
3. Select the **Candidates** tab.
4. Search for the candidate using their name or email address.
5. Select the candidate from the list.
6. Click **Resend Invite**.
7. Review the candidate's email address and confirm the invitation details.
8. Click **Send** to resend the invitation.
9. Ask the candidate to check their inbox and spam or junk folders for the latest invitation.

**Note:** Ask the candidate to use the most recent invitation email. Older invitation links may no longer provide access if the candidate's invitation or test availability has changed.

If the candidate still does not receive the email, verify that the address is correct and that emails from HackerRank are not being filtered by the candidate's mail provider. For cases where the candidate receives the invitation but cannot access the test, see [📄 Test Link Says Unauthorized](/articles/7418263095).
