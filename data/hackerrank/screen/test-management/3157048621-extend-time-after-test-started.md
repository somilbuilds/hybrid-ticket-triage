# Extend Time After Test Started

_Last updated: September 1, 2026, 3:20 PM (Last updated 16 days ago)_

You can extend the remaining test time for a candidate when additional time is required after the assessment has started. This article explains how to locate the candidate attempt and update the available time.

# Prerequisites

- You must have permission to manage the test and candidate attempts.
- The candidate must have an active test attempt.
- Confirm the additional time that should be provided.

# Extending the candidate's time

To extend the test time:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Tests** and open the relevant test.
3. Select **Candidates** and locate the candidate.
4. Open the candidate's active attempt or assessment details.
5. Select the available **Extend Time** option.
6. Enter the additional time to provide to the candidate.
7. Review the updated time limit and click **Save**.
8. Ask the candidate to continue the active test session.

**Note:** Extending the time limit does not restart the candidate's test or reset completed questions. If the candidate has already submitted the test, the remaining time cannot be restored through this workflow.

The updated time should be reflected in the candidate's active assessment. If the candidate does not see the additional time, ask them to refresh the assessment page without closing the active session. For related candidate controls, see [📄 Managing Candidate Test Attempts](/articles/5842107631).
