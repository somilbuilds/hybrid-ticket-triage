# Reset a Candidate Test Attempt

_Last updated: August 19, 2026, 9:40 AM (Last updated 4 weeks ago)_

Reset a candidate attempt when your assessment workflow permits a fresh attempt after a technical issue or an approved exception. Review the existing attempt before making the change.

# Prerequisites

- Permission to manage candidate attempts.
- The candidate's test and attempt details.
- Approval to provide another attempt when required by your assessment process.

# Reset the attempt

To reset a candidate's attempt:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Tests** and select the relevant assessment.
3. Select **Candidates** and locate the candidate.
4. Open the candidate's current attempt.
5. Review the attempt status and existing results.
6. Select the available **Reset Attempt** or equivalent action.
7. Review the warning or confirmation message before proceeding.
8. Confirm the reset and verify that the candidate receives the expected access to a new attempt.

**Note:** Resetting an attempt can affect previously recorded progress or results. Preserve any information required for your recruiting records before confirming the action.

If the candidate needs additional time instead, see [📄 Extend Time After Test Started](/articles/3157048621).
