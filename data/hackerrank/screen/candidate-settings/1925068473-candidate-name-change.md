# Change Candidate Name

_Last updated: August 20, 2026, 11:10 AM (Last updated 4 weeks ago)_

Correct a candidate's name when an invitation was created with a spelling error or when the candidate's profile information needs to match your recruiting records.

# Prerequisites

- Permission to manage candidates in **HackerRank for Work**.
- The candidate's correct first and last name.
- Access to the relevant test or candidate record.

# Update candidate information

To update a candidate's name:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Tests** and select the assessment containing the candidate.
3. Select **Candidates** and search for the candidate.
4. Open the candidate's profile or attempt details.
5. Select **Edit** or the available candidate information option.
6. Update the candidate's name to match your recruiting records.
7. Select **Save**.
8. Reopen the candidate record and verify that the corrected name is displayed.

**Note:** Changing a candidate's display information does not change the email address used for authentication. If the email address is incorrect, use the candidate email update workflow instead.

For email corrections, see [📄 Change Candidate Email After Invite](/articles/4268159370).
