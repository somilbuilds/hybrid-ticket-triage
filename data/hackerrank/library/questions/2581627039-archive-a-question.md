# Archive a Question

_Last updated: August 14, 2026, 11:25 AM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

Archive questions that should no longer be used in new assessments while preserving the question record for reference where supported. Review dependencies before archiving shared content.

# Prerequisites

- Permission to manage the question library in **HackerRank for Work**.
- The question you want to archive.
- Confirmation that the question is not required by an active assessment workflow.

# Archive a question

To archive a question:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Library** and locate the question.
3. Open the question details.
4. Review where the question is currently used or referenced.
5. Select the available **Archive** action.
6. Review the confirmation message.
7. Confirm the archive action.
8. Search the library to verify that the question is no longer presented as an active question for new use.

**Note:** Archiving is different from deleting a question. Keep a record of why content was archived so other administrators do not reintroduce outdated or duplicate content.

For duplicate content warnings, see [📄 Duplicate Question Warning](/articles/2074968351).
