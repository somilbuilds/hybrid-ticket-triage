# Workday Candidate Result Not Updating

_Last updated: September 2, 2026, 1:50 PM (Last updated 15 days ago)_

This article explains how to troubleshoot a candidate result that has not appeared or updated in Workday after an assessment in HackerRank for Work.

# Prerequisites

- The Workday integration must be configured for your HackerRank for Work account.
- You must have permission to manage or review the integration.
- Confirm that the candidate has completed or submitted the assessment.

# Troubleshooting result synchronization

To troubleshoot a candidate result that is not updating:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Settings** and select **Integrations**.
3. Open the **Workday** integration and review its connection status.
4. Confirm that the candidate's email or identifier matches the record in Workday.
5. Verify that the candidate has a completed assessment result in HackerRank for Work.
6. Review the integration or synchronization history for errors.
7. Confirm that the Workday connection is authorized and has the required permissions.
8. If authorization has expired, use the available **Reconnect** or authorization option.
9. Refresh the candidate record in Workday and verify whether the result has been updated.

**Note:** A completed assessment in HackerRank for Work does not necessarily mean that the corresponding Workday record has been updated if the integration is disconnected or the candidate mapping is incorrect.

If the result remains missing, record the candidate identifier, assessment name, completion time, and synchronization error before contacting support. See [📄 Troubleshoot ATS Synchronization](/articles/5837192640).
