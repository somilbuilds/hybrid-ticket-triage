# ATS Stage Missing HackerRank Option

_Last updated: September 2, 2026, 3:15 PM (Last updated 15 days ago)_

This article explains what to check when a HackerRank for Work action is not available for a candidate at an expected stage in an applicant tracking system.

# Prerequisites

- The ATS integration must be enabled and connected.
- You must have permission to manage the ATS configuration.
- Confirm that the affected job and candidate are available in the ATS.

# Checking the ATS stage configuration

To troubleshoot a missing HackerRank option:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Settings** and select **Integrations**.
3. Open the configured ATS integration and verify that its connection is active.
4. Confirm that the affected job is included in the integration configuration.
5. Review the candidate's current stage in the ATS.
6. Verify that the HackerRank action is configured for that stage.
7. If the candidate was recently moved between stages, allow the integration to synchronize.
8. Refresh the candidate record and check the available actions again.
9. If the option is still missing, review the integration permissions and job configuration.

**Note:** ATS actions can depend on the candidate's current job, stage, and integration configuration. A candidate moved to another stage may no longer display the HackerRank action configured for the previous stage.

If the option remains unavailable, record the ATS name, job, candidate stage, and integration status before contacting support. See [📄 Configure ATS Workflow Stages](/articles/7294163058).
