# Reconnect an ATS Integration

_Last updated: August 18, 2026, 2:45 PM (Last updated 4 weeks ago)_

Reconnect an applicant tracking system when its authorization has expired, credentials changed, or the integration no longer synchronizes records.

# Prerequisites

- Administrator access to **HackerRank for Work**.
- Administrator access to the connected ATS.
- Current authorization or credentials required by the ATS.

# Reconnect the integration

To reconnect an ATS:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Integrations** and select the affected ATS.
3. Review the connection status and identify any authorization error.
4. Select the available **Reconnect**, **Authorize**, or equivalent action.
5. Complete the authentication flow using the ATS administrator account.
6. Confirm that the integration reports a connected status.
7. Test synchronization with a controlled candidate or job record.
8. Verify that the expected candidate or assessment information appears in both systems.

**Note:** Reconnecting an integration does not automatically correct data that failed to synchronize while the connection was unavailable. Review recent failed events and retry supported operations after reconnection.

For candidate synchronization problems, see [📄 Troubleshoot ATS Candidate Sync](/articles/9465738264).
