# ATS Job Is Not Syncing

_Last updated: August 4, 2026, 3:20 PM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

When a job from an applicant tracking system does not appear in HackerRank for Work, verify the integration connection, job status, and synchronization rules.

# Prerequisites

- Administrator access to the ATS integration.
- Access to the affected job in the ATS.
- Permission to review integration activity.

# Troubleshoot job synchronization

To investigate a missing job:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Integrations** and select the connected ATS.
3. Confirm that the integration is connected and active.
4. Open the affected job in the ATS and verify its status and required fields.
5. Check whether the job meets the integration's synchronization criteria.
6. Review recent integration activity for an error associated with the job.
7. Correct the job or integration configuration when required.
8. Retry synchronization and confirm that the job appears in **HackerRank for Work**.

**Note:** A job may not synchronize simply because it exists in the ATS. Integration workflows can restrict synchronization by status, stage, permissions, or configured job filters.

For general ATS troubleshooting, see [📄 Troubleshoot ATS Candidate Sync](/articles/9465738264).
