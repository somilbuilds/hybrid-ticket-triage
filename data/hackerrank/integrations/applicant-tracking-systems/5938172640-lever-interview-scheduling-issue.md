# Lever Interview Scheduling Issue

_Last updated: September 4, 2026, 3:05 PM (Last updated 13 days ago)_

This article explains how to troubleshoot interview scheduling issues between Lever and HackerRank for Work. Check the integration connection, candidate mapping, and interview configuration when a scheduled interview does not appear as expected.

# Prerequisites

- The Lever integration must be enabled for your HackerRank for Work account.
- You must have access to the relevant candidate or interview.
- Confirm that the candidate and job exist in Lever.

# Troubleshooting interview scheduling

To troubleshoot a scheduling issue:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Settings** and select **Integrations**.
3. Open the **Lever** integration and verify that the connection is active.
4. Confirm that the candidate is associated with the expected job or requisition in Lever.
5. Review the interview details and verify the scheduled date, time, and participants.
6. Check whether the Lever authorization has expired or been disconnected.
7. Review any synchronization errors displayed by the integration.
8. If required, use **Reconnect** or the available authorization option to restore the connection.
9. Return to the interview schedule in HackerRank for Work and verify that the interview is present.

**Note:** Candidate and job mapping must be consistent between Lever and HackerRank for Work. A scheduling issue can occur when a candidate is moved to a different job stage or the integration no longer has access to the relevant record.

If the interview still does not appear, record the candidate, job, scheduled time, and exact error message before contacting support. See [📄 Configure Lever Integration](/articles/7164295830).
