# Greenhouse Sync Failed

_Last updated: September 1, 2026, 4:30 PM (Last updated 16 days ago)_

This article explains how to troubleshoot a failed synchronization between HackerRank for Work and Greenhouse. It covers common connection, permission, and configuration issues that can prevent candidate or job data from syncing.

# Prerequisites

- The Greenhouse integration must be enabled for your HackerRank for Work account.
- You must have administrator permissions for the integration.
- Confirm that the relevant job or candidate exists in Greenhouse.

# Troubleshooting the Greenhouse sync

To troubleshoot a failed sync:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Settings** and open **Integrations**.
3. Select **Greenhouse** and review the current connection status.
4. Verify that the integration credentials or authorization are still valid.
5. Confirm that the Greenhouse user or API credentials used by the integration have the required permissions.
6. Check that the relevant Greenhouse job is active and configured for integration with HackerRank for Work.
7. Review any error message displayed in the integration settings or synchronization history.
8. If the connection has expired, use **Reconnect** or **Authorize** to establish the connection again.
9. Retry the synchronization and confirm that the candidate or job record is updated.

**Note:** A sync can fail when Greenhouse permissions have changed or when the authorization used by the integration is no longer valid. Reauthorizing the integration does not change existing job or candidate configuration.

If the sync continues to fail, record the exact error message and the affected Greenhouse job or candidate before contacting support. See [📄 Configure the Greenhouse Integration](/articles/3158274069) for integration setup requirements.
