# Reassign a SkillUp Assignment

_Last updated: August 10, 2026, 3:20 PM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

Reassign a SkillUp assignment when responsibility for a learner changes or an assignment needs to move to another learner or manager scope.

# Prerequisites

- Manager or administrator access to **HackerRank for Work** SkillUp.
- The existing assignment.
- The correct learner or assignment owner.

# Reassign an assignment

To reassign a SkillUp assignment:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **SkillUp** and locate the relevant assignment.
3. Open the assignment details.
4. Select the available **Reassign** or edit option.
5. Search for the new learner or select the appropriate recipient.
6. Review the assignment deadline and completion requirements.
7. Select **Save** or **Reassign**.
8. Verify that the assignment appears under the new learner's assigned work.

**Note:** Reassignment can change who is responsible for completing the work, but it may not erase progress already recorded for the original learner. Review the assignment history after making the change.

For learning-path assignments, see [📄 Assign Learning Path](/articles/7136502849).
