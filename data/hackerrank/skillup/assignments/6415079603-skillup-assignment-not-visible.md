# SkillUp Assignment Is Not Visible

_Last updated: July 11, 2026, 1:05 PM (Last updated 2 months ago)_

If a learner cannot see an assigned SkillUp activity, verify the learner's assignment status, account access, and the scope of the learning path.

# Prerequisites

- Manager or administrator access to **HackerRank for Work** SkillUp.
- The learner's email address.
- The relevant assignment or learning path.

# Troubleshoot a missing assignment

To investigate a missing assignment:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **SkillUp** and locate the relevant assignment or learning path.
3. Search for the learner and confirm that the assignment was created for the correct account.
4. Review the assignment status, start date, and deadline.
5. Confirm that the learner has an active **HackerRank for Work** account.
6. Ask the learner to refresh SkillUp or sign in again.
7. Confirm that the learner is in the correct team or reporting scope when the assignment is team-based.
8. Reassign or resend the assignment when required.

**Note:** A learner may have an account but still lack access to a specific assignment because the assignment was created for another learner, team, or path.

For reassignment, see [📄 Reassign a SkillUp Assignment](/articles/2925061473).
