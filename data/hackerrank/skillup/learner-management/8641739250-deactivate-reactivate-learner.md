# Deactivate and Reactivate Learner

_Last updated: September 3, 2026, 12:40 PM (Last updated 14 days ago)_

You can deactivate a learner in HackerRank for Work when the user should temporarily lose access to SkillUp content. Reactivate the learner when access should be restored.

# Prerequisites

- You must have permission to manage learners.
- Confirm the learner's email address or user record.
- Verify that deactivation is appropriate for the learner.

# Deactivating a learner

To deactivate a learner:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **SkillUp** and open **Learners** or the learner management area.
3. Search for the learner by name or email address.
4. Open the learner's profile.
5. Select **Deactivate** or the available account status option.
6. Confirm the action.
7. Verify that the learner's status is displayed as inactive.

# Reactivating a learner

To restore access:

1. Open the learner's profile from **Learners**.
2. Select **Reactivate** or the available status option.
3. Confirm the action.
4. Verify that the learner can access SkillUp content again.

**Note:** Deactivating a learner may affect access to assigned learning paths and other SkillUp content. Review the learner's existing assignments before changing account status.

If the learner cannot access content after reactivation, verify organization membership, team assignment, and learning path assignment. See [📄 Assign Learning Path](/articles/1846372950).
