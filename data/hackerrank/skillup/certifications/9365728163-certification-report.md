# Certification Report

_Last updated: August 26, 2026, 1:25 PM (Last updated 3 weeks ago)_

Use certification reporting to review learner certification status, completion details, and available certification records. Reports can help managers identify completed certifications and learners who still need to finish required work.

# Prerequisites

- Manager or reporting access to **HackerRank for Work** SkillUp.
- Learners with certification activity or assignments.
- A certification program or reporting scope to review.

# View certification results

To review certification data:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **SkillUp** and navigate to the certification or reporting area.
3. Select the relevant **Certification** or reporting view.
4. Filter the results by learner, team, certification, or status when available.
5. Review certification status and completion details.
6. Open an individual learner record when you need to investigate a specific result.
7. Export the report if an export option is available and your permissions allow it.
8. Use the report to identify completed, in-progress, and outstanding certification requirements.

**Note:** Reporting data may take time to reflect recently completed learner activity. If a result appears incomplete immediately after a learner finishes an activity, refresh the report after the activity has been processed.

For broader learner reporting, see [📄 Manager Dashboard](/articles/9476839274).
