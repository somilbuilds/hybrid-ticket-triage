# Certification Has Expired

_Last updated: July 10, 2026, 11:45 AM (Last updated 2 months ago)_

A certification can become unavailable or show as expired when its validity period has ended. Review the certification record and program requirements before assigning replacement work.

# Prerequisites

- Manager or administrator access to **HackerRank for Work** SkillUp.
- The learner's certification record.
- The organization's renewal or recertification policy.

# Review an expired certification

To investigate an expired certification:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **SkillUp** and navigate to the learner's certification record.
3. Confirm the certification name and expiration date.
4. Review whether the certification supports renewal or requires a new certification attempt.
5. Check the organization's current certification requirements.
6. Assign the required renewal or replacement learning activity when applicable.
7. Verify the learner receives the new assignment.
8. Review the certification report after the learner completes the required work.

**Note:** Do not manually treat an expired certification as current unless your organization's certification policy explicitly permits an extension.

For reporting, see [📄 Certification Report](/articles/9365728163).
