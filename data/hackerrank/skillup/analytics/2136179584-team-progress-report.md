# Team Progress Report

_Last updated: August 9, 2026, 11:35 AM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

Use team progress reporting to review learning activity across a group of learners. Filter the report to identify completion gaps and compare progress against assigned learning requirements.

# Prerequisites

- Manager or reporting access to **HackerRank for Work** SkillUp.
- A team with assigned learners.
- Learning paths or assignments associated with the team.

# Review team progress

To review a team report:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **SkillUp** and navigate to the reporting or analytics area.
3. Select the relevant **Team Progress** report.
4. Choose the team or manager scope you want to review.
5. Filter by learning path, assignment status, learner, or date when available.
6. Review completion, in-progress, and outstanding activity.
7. Open individual learner records to investigate exceptions.
8. Export the report when an export option is available and you need to share the results.

**Note:** Report totals may change as learner activity is processed. Use the same filters and date range when comparing reports from different points in time.

For dashboard-based reporting, see [📄 Manager Dashboard](/articles/9587941385).
