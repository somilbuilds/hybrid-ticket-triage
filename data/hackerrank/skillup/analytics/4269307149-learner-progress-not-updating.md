# Learner Progress Is Not Updating

_Last updated: July 26, 2026, 1:55 PM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

If a learner's SkillUp progress appears unchanged after completing an activity, verify the activity status first and then check whether reporting data has finished processing.

# Prerequisites

- Manager or administrator access to **HackerRank for Work** SkillUp.
- The affected learner's email address.
- The learning path or assignment being reviewed.

# Troubleshoot progress

To investigate stale learner progress:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **SkillUp** and locate the learner's assignment or learning path.
3. Open the individual activity and confirm its completion status.
4. Check the activity's completion time and any required passing condition.
5. Return to the learner or manager reporting view and refresh the data.
6. Confirm that the learner is still assigned to the expected path or activity.
7. Compare the learner's individual status with the team progress report.
8. If the status remains incorrect, record the learner, activity, completion time, and displayed status for investigation.

**Note:** Individual activity status and aggregate dashboard metrics can update at different times. Verify the source activity before treating a reporting delay as a failed completion.

For team-level reporting, see [📄 Team Progress Report](/articles/2136179584).
