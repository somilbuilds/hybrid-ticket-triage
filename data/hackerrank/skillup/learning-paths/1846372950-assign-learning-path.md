# Assign Learning Path

_Last updated: August 29, 2026, 12:35 PM (Last updated 19 days ago)_

You can assign a learning path to users or groups in HackerRank for Work. Assignments provide learners with access to a structured sequence of learning content and allow administrators to track progress.

# Prerequisites

- You must have permission to manage SkillUp content and assignments.
- The learning path must be available in your organization's library.
- The learners must belong to your HackerRank for Work organization.

# Assigning a learning path

To assign a learning path:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **SkillUp** and select **Learning Paths**.
3. Open the learning path you want to assign.
4. Click **Assign**.
5. Select the users, groups, or teams that should receive the assignment.
6. Configure the **Due Date** if learners must complete the path by a specific date.
7. Review the selected learners and assignment settings.
8. Click **Assign Learning Path** to confirm.
9. Open the assignment details and verify that the selected learners are listed.

**Note:** A learning path cannot be assigned to users who are not members of the organization. Add the users to the appropriate organization or team before creating the assignment.

After the assignment is created, learners can access the learning path from their SkillUp dashboard. Administrators can use the assignment details to review learner progress and completion status. For additional information, see [📄 Manage Learning Paths](/articles/5274186390).
