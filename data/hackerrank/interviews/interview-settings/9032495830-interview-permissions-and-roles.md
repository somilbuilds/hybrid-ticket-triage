# Interview Permissions and Roles

_Last updated: August 29, 2026, 11:05 AM (Last updated 3 weeks ago)_

Interview access depends on the user's assigned role and the permissions granted by the organization. Review these settings when an interviewer cannot access an interview, scorecard, or related resource.

# Prerequisites

- Administrator access to user or interview permissions.
- The interviewer's email address.
- The affected interview or resource.

# Review interviewer access

To check interview permissions:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Settings** and select **User Management**.
3. Search for the interviewer by email address.
4. Review the user's assigned **Role** and available permissions.
5. Confirm that the role includes access to the required interview resources.
6. Open the affected interview and verify that the interviewer is assigned to the session when applicable.
7. Update the user's role or interview assignment if the access is incorrect.
8. Ask the interviewer to sign out and sign back in before testing access again.

**Note:** Changing a user's organization role can affect access beyond a single interview. Prefer an interview-specific assignment when that provides the required access.

For interviewer access problems, see [📄 Interviewer Cannot Join](/articles/4628195730).
