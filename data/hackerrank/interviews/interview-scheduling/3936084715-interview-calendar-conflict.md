# Interview Calendar Conflict

_Last updated: July 29, 2026, 9:35 AM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

A calendar conflict can occur when an interviewer is already unavailable or when the interview time does not match the availability shown by the connected scheduling system.

# Prerequisites

- Access to the affected interview in **HackerRank for Work**.
- Access to the interviewer's calendar when required.
- Permission to reschedule interviews.

# Resolve a calendar conflict

To investigate a scheduling conflict:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Interviews** and select the affected interview.
3. Review the scheduled date, time zone, and participants.
4. Check the interviewer's calendar for an overlapping event or unavailable period.
5. Confirm that the calendar integration is connected and current.
6. Select another available time when the conflict is confirmed.
7. Save the updated schedule and verify the participant list.
8. Confirm that updated invitations or calendar events are sent as expected.

**Note:** Always check the time zone displayed for the interview before concluding that two events overlap. A time-zone mismatch can make otherwise valid schedules appear conflicting.

For changing the scheduled time, see [📄 Reschedule Interview](/articles/5317496280).
