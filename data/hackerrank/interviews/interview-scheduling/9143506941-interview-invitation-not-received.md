# Interview Invitation Not Received

_Last updated: August 28, 2026, 4:00 PM (Last updated 3 weeks ago)_

If an interviewer or candidate does not receive an interview invitation, verify the recipient address, interview schedule, and email delivery status before resending the invitation.

# Prerequisites

- Access to the scheduled interview in **HackerRank for Work**.
- The recipient's email address.
- Permission to manage interview scheduling.

# Troubleshoot the invitation

To investigate a missing invitation:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Interviews** and select the affected interview.
3. Review the candidate and interviewer email addresses for spelling errors.
4. Confirm that the interview has a valid date, time, and assigned participants.
5. Review the interview's invitation or notification status.
6. Ask the recipient to check spam, junk, quarantine, and corporate email filtering.
7. Confirm that the recipient's organization allows messages from the configured HackerRank for Work email sender.
8. Resend the invitation after correcting any incorrect details.

**Note:** Resending an invitation does not fix an incorrect participant email address. Update the recipient information first and then send the invitation again.

For interview timing changes, see [📄 Reschedule Interview](/articles/5317496280).
