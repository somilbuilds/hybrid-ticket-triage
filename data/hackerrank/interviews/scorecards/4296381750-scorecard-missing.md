# Scorecard Missing

_Last updated: September 2, 2026, 2:05 PM (Last updated 15 days ago)_

This article explains how to troubleshoot a missing interview scorecard in HackerRank for Work. Verify the interview assignment, interviewer access, and scorecard configuration.

# Prerequisites

- The interview must exist in HackerRank for Work.
- You must have permission to view the interview or scorecard.
- Confirm the interviewer was assigned to the interview.

# Troubleshooting a missing scorecard

To troubleshoot a missing scorecard:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Interviews** and open the relevant interview.
3. Review the **Interviewers** section and confirm that the interviewer is assigned.
4. Open the interview's **Scorecard** or feedback area.
5. Verify that a scorecard template is configured for the interview.
6. Confirm that the interviewer is using the correct account and interview session.
7. If the interview has ended, refresh the interview details and check the completed feedback.
8. Verify that your role has permission to view interview feedback and scorecards.

**Note:** A scorecard may not be visible when no scorecard template was configured, the interviewer was not assigned, or the current user does not have permission to view the interview feedback.

If the scorecard is configured but remains unavailable, record the interview ID, interviewer, and interview status before contacting support. See [📄 Interview Feedback Not Saving](/articles/5417296830).
