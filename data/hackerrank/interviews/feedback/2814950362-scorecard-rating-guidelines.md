# Configure Scorecard Rating Guidelines

_Last updated: August 11, 2026, 10:45 AM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

Rating guidelines help interviewers apply consistent criteria when completing scorecards. Define clear expectations for each competency and rating level used by your hiring workflow.

# Prerequisites

- Permission to manage interview scorecards in **HackerRank for Work**.
- Defined competencies for the interview type.
- Agreed rating criteria from the recruiting team.

# Update rating guidelines

To configure scorecard guidance:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Interviews** and navigate to the relevant **Scorecard** configuration.
3. Select the interview type or scorecard you want to update.
4. Review the existing competencies and rating fields.
5. Add or edit guidance that explains what each rating level represents.
6. Keep descriptions specific enough for interviewers to apply consistently.
7. Select **Save**.
8. Run a test interview or review a sample scorecard to confirm that the guidance is visible as expected.

**Note:** Rating guidelines should describe observable interview evidence rather than assumptions about a candidate. Keep the criteria aligned with the role and interview structure.

For missing feedback, see [📄 Interview Feedback Not Saving](/articles/6428517390).
