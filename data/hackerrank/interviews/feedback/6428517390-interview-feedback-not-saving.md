# Interview Feedback Not Saving

_Last updated: September 5, 2026, 3:35 PM (Last updated 12 days ago)_

This article explains how to troubleshoot interview feedback that does not save in HackerRank for Work. Check interviewer access, required scorecard fields, and the active interview session.

# Prerequisites

- The interviewer must have access to the interview.
- The interview must still allow feedback to be submitted.
- Confirm that all required scorecard fields have been completed.

# Troubleshooting feedback

To troubleshoot feedback that does not save:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Interviews** and open the relevant interview.
3. Open the **Feedback** or **Scorecard** section.
4. Review the scorecard and complete any fields marked as required.
5. Verify that you are signed in with the interviewer account assigned to the interview.
6. Click **Save** or **Submit Feedback** and wait for the confirmation.
7. If the page does not respond, refresh the page and reopen the interview.
8. Re-enter any unsaved feedback and submit it again.
9. Verify that the saved feedback appears in the interview record.

**Note:** Refreshing a page with unsaved feedback can discard changes. Copy important feedback before refreshing if the page is not responding.

If feedback still cannot be saved, record the interview ID, interviewer email, browser, and exact error message before contacting support. See [📄 Scorecard Missing](/articles/4296381750).
