# Check Scorecard Completion Status

_Last updated: July 12, 2026, 9:50 AM (Last updated 2 months ago)_

Use scorecard completion status to identify interviewers who still need to submit required feedback after an interview.

# Prerequisites

- Permission to view interview feedback in **HackerRank for Work**.
- Access to the affected interview.
- The interviewer's name or email address.

# Review scorecard status

To check scorecard completion:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Interviews** and select the completed interview.
3. Open the **Feedback** or **Scorecards** section.
4. Review the assigned interviewers and their submission status.
5. Identify scorecards that are incomplete or still pending.
6. Open the relevant scorecard to determine whether required fields remain empty.
7. Contact the interviewer or resend the feedback request when supported.
8. Refresh the interview record after the interviewer submits the scorecard.

**Note:** An interviewer can participate in an interview without having submitted a completed scorecard. Check completion status rather than assuming participation means feedback was recorded.

For missing scorecards, see [📄 Scorecard Missing](/articles/4296381750).
