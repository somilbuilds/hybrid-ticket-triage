# Reschedule Interview

_Last updated: September 1, 2026, 1:15 PM (Last updated 16 days ago)_

You can reschedule a scheduled HackerRank for Work interview when the candidate or interviewer is unavailable. Review the new time, participants, and time zone before saving the change.

# Prerequisites

- The interview must be scheduled and not canceled.
- You must have permission to manage the interview.
- Confirm the new date and time with the required participants.

# Rescheduling an interview

To reschedule an interview:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Interviews** and open the interview you want to reschedule.
3. Select **Edit**, **Reschedule**, or the available scheduling option.
4. Choose the new **Date** and **Time**.
5. Verify the displayed time zone.
6. Review the candidate and interviewer details.
7. Save the updated interview schedule.
8. Verify that the interview now appears at the new time.
9. Notify the participants using the available notification or invitation workflow.

**Note:** Rescheduling an interview does not necessarily update external calendar events that were created outside HackerRank for Work. Verify the participants' calendars when the interview is connected to an external scheduling workflow.

If the new time cannot be selected, check interviewer availability and any scheduling restrictions configured for the interview. See [📄 Schedule and Manage Interviews](/articles/6048219375).
