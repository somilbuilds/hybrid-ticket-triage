# Interviewer Cannot Join

_Last updated: September 4, 2026, 1:25 PM (Last updated 13 days ago)_

This article explains how to troubleshoot an interviewer who cannot join a scheduled HackerRank for Work interview.

# Prerequisites

- Confirm that the interview has been scheduled.
- The interviewer must be added to the interview or have a valid interview invitation.
- Verify that the interview has not been canceled.

# Troubleshooting interviewer access

To help an interviewer join an interview:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Interviews** and open the scheduled interview.
3. Review the **Interviewers** section and confirm that the interviewer is listed.
4. Verify that the interviewer's email address is correct.
5. Confirm the interview's scheduled date and time, including the applicable time zone.
6. Ask the interviewer to open the interview from the latest invitation or from their **Interviews** dashboard.
7. If the interviewer does not have access, remove and re-add the interviewer to the interview.
8. Ask the interviewer to sign in with the same email address associated with the interview invitation.
9. If the join page does not load, ask the interviewer to refresh the page or use a supported browser.

**Note:** An interviewer may be unable to join when they are not assigned to the interview, are signed in with a different account, or are using an outdated invitation link.

If the interview has already started, verify that the interviewer is using the active interview session rather than a previous meeting link. For additional scheduling information, see [📄 Schedule an Interview](/articles/5816249037).
