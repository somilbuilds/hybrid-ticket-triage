# Interviewer Audio and Video Issue

_Last updated: September 4, 2026, 12:35 PM (Last updated 13 days ago)_

This article explains how to troubleshoot audio or video problems for an interviewer in a HackerRank for Work interview. Check browser permissions, device selection, and other applications using the microphone or camera.

# Prerequisites

- The interviewer must be assigned to the interview.
- The interviewer should use a supported browser.
- A working microphone and camera should be available.

# Troubleshooting audio and video

To troubleshoot an interviewer's audio or video:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Open **Interviews** and select the active interview.
3. Confirm that the interviewer is using the correct interview session.
4. Check the browser's permission settings and allow access to the microphone and camera.
5. Verify that the correct microphone and camera are selected in the interview interface.
6. Close other applications that may be using the microphone or camera.
7. Refresh the interview page and rejoin the session if necessary.
8. If the issue continues, restart the browser and rejoin using the interview dashboard.
9. Test the microphone and camera outside the interview to confirm that the devices are working.

**Note:** Operating system privacy settings can prevent the browser from accessing audio or video devices even when browser permissions appear enabled.

If the interviewer still cannot use audio or video, record the browser, device, selected input and output devices, and exact error message before contacting support. See [📄 Interviewer Cannot Join](/articles/9735261840).
