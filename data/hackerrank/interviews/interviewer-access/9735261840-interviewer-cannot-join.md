# Interviewer Cannot Join

_Last updated: September 5, 2026, 1:10 PM (Last updated 12 days ago)_

This article explains how to troubleshoot an interviewer who cannot join a scheduled HackerRank for Work interview. Verify the interviewer assignment, interview status, and account used to access the session.

# Prerequisites

- The interview must be scheduled in HackerRank for Work.
- The interviewer must be assigned to the interview.
- Confirm that the interview has not been canceled.

# Troubleshooting interviewer access

To help an interviewer join an interview:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials.
2. Navigate to **Interviews** and open the scheduled interview.
3. Review the **Interviewers** section and confirm that the interviewer is listed.
4. Verify the interviewer's email address.
5. Check the scheduled date, time, and time zone.
6. Ask the interviewer to open the session from the latest invitation or their **Interviews** dashboard.
7. Confirm that the interviewer is signed in with the same email address used for the invitation.
8. If the interviewer is not listed, use the available option to add the interviewer to the interview.
9. If the join page does not load, ask the interviewer to refresh the page or try a supported browser.

**Note:** An interviewer may not be able to join if they are not assigned to the interview or are signed in with a different account. An old interview link may also point to an inactive session.

If the interview has already started, verify that the interviewer is joining the active session rather than a previous scheduled session. See [📄 Schedule and Manage Interviews](/articles/6048219375).
