# Clear Browser Cache and Cookies

_Last updated: July 25, 2026, 9:10 AM (Last updated 1 month ago)_

Clearing cached browser data can resolve stale pages, repeated sign-in prompts, or outdated interface behavior in HackerRank for Work. Preserve any unsaved work before clearing site data.

# Prerequisites

- An updated supported browser.
- Access to your **HackerRank for Work** account.
- Any unsaved work copied or saved elsewhere.

# Clear cached data

To troubleshoot browser-related issues:

1. Log in to your **HackerRank for Work** account using your credentials when possible.
2. Save any work that you do not want to lose.
3. Open the browser's privacy or site-data settings.
4. Clear cached files and cookies for the affected HackerRank for Work site when supported.
5. Close the affected browser tabs.
6. Reopen the browser and navigate to HackerRank for Work.
7. Sign in again and retry the action that previously failed.
8. If the issue persists, test the same action in another supported browser and record the result.

**Note:** Clearing cookies can sign you out of other sessions and may remove site-specific preferences. Use site-specific clearing when your browser supports it.
